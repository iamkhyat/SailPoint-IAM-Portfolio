# 🧩 Access Profile Design

## 🎯 Objective
Design scalable and business-aligned access profiles in IdentityNow.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario
Large enterprise with:
- Thousands of entitlements
- Multiple departments

Needs:
- Simplified access management

---

## 🧠 IAM Design Approach

👉 Group entitlements based on:
- Job function
- Department
- Application usage

---

## 🔑 Key SailPoint Concepts Used

- Access Profiles
- Entitlements
- Least Privilege

---

## ⚙️ Step-by-Step Flow

1. Analyze entitlements  
2. Group logically  
3. Create profile  
4. Validate with business  

---

## ⚠️ Common Issues

- Too many profiles  
- Incorrect grouping  

---

## 🎤 Interview Talking Points

- Role explosion vs simplification  
- Business alignment  

---

## 🚀 Key Takeaways

- Design impacts scalability  
- Keep profiles simple
