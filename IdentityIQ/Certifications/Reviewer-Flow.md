# 👥 Reviewer Flow

## 🎯 Objective
Explain how reviewers perform access certifications in IdentityIQ.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario
Managers or application owners must validate access:
- Do users still need access?
- Is access appropriate?

---

## 🧠 IAM Design Approach
- Assign reviews to responsible users
- Provide clear visibility of access
- Enable approve/revoke decisions

---

## 🔑 Key SailPoint Concepts Used
- Reviewer Assignment
- Certification UI
- Decision Actions
- Delegation

---

## ⚙️ Step-by-Step Flow

1. Reviewer receives notification  
2. Logs into IdentityIQ  
3. Views assigned certification items  
4. Reviews access details  
5. Takes action:
   - Approve
   - Revoke
6. Submits decision  

---

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Troubleshooting
- Reviewer confusion → Poor UI clarity  
- Delays → Lack of reminders  
- Incorrect decisions → Insufficient data  

---

## 🎤 Interview Talking Points
- Reviewer experience  
- Approval vs revocation  
- Delegation process
