# 🧩 Certification Campaign Design

## 🎯 Objective
Explain how to design effective certification campaigns in IdentityIQ.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario
Organization needs periodic reviews:
- Quarterly SOX audits
- Application-specific reviews
- High-risk access validation

---

## 🧠 IAM Design Approach
- Define scope carefully
- Assign correct reviewers
- Schedule campaigns periodically
- Focus on risk-based reviews

---

## 🔑 Key SailPoint Concepts Used
- Campaign Types
- Certification Scope
- Scheduling
- Reviewer Assignment

---

## ⚙️ Step-by-Step Flow

1. Select campaign type (Manager/App Owner)  
2. Define scope (users, roles, apps)  
3. Configure schedule  
4. Assign reviewers  
5. Set deadlines  
6. Launch campaign  

---

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Troubleshooting
- Too many items → Reviewer overload  
- Wrong scope → Irrelevant reviews  
- Missing reviewers → Campaign failure  

---

## 🎤 Interview Talking Points
- Campaign types  
- Risk-based certifications  
- Scheduling strategy
