# 📊 Audit & Compliance Considerations

## 🎯 Objective
Explain how certifications support audit and compliance requirements.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario
Organizations must comply with:
- SOX
- GDPR
- Internal security policies

Auditors require proof of:
- Access reviews
- Decision tracking
- Remediation actions

---

## 🧠 IAM Design Approach
- Maintain audit logs
- Track certification decisions
- Provide reports for auditors
- Ensure policy enforcement

---

## 🔑 Key SailPoint Concepts Used
- Audit Logs
- Certification Reports
- Policies (SoD)
- Compliance Tracking

---

## ⚙️ Step-by-Step Flow

1. Campaign executed  
2. Decisions recorded  
3. Revocations processed  
4. Audit logs stored  
5. Reports generated  

---

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Troubleshooting
- Missing logs → Misconfiguration  
- Incomplete campaigns → Compliance risk  
- No evidence → Audit failure  

---

## 🎤 Interview Talking Points
- How certifications support compliance  
- Importance of audit logs  
- Reporting for auditors
