# 🔐 Entitlement Mapping

## 🎯 Objective
Convert technical entitlements into business-friendly roles.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario
Applications provide:
- Raw permissions (e.g., READ_DB)

Business needs:
- Understandable access

---

## 🧠 IAM Design Approach
- Group entitlements
- Create roles (RBAC)
- Simplify access management

---

## 🔑 Key SailPoint Concepts Used
- Entitlements
- Roles
- RBAC

---

## ⚙️ Step-by-Step Flow

1. Analyze entitlements  
2. Group logically  
3. Create IT roles  
4. Map to business roles  
5. Assign to users  

---

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Troubleshooting
- Role explosion  
- Incorrect grouping  
- Overlapping roles  

---

## 🎤 Interview Talking Points
- RBAC importance  
- Role design strategy  
- Entitlement grouping
