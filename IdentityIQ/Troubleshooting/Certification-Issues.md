# 🛡️ Certification Issues

## 🎯 Objective
Identify and resolve issues related to access certification campaigns.

---

## 🏢 Business Scenario
Organizations conduct access reviews:
- Managers review user access
- Access is approved or revoked

Failures impact compliance.

---

## 🧠 IAM Design Approach
- Ensure correct campaign configuration
- Assign proper reviewers
- Track remediation

---

## 🔑 Key SailPoint Concepts Used
- Certification Campaigns  
- Reviewers  
- Revocation Requests  
- Audit Logs  

---

## ⚙️ Step-by-Step Flow

1. Campaign created  
2. Reviewers assigned  
3. Access reviewed  
4. Decisions made  
5. Remediation executed  

---

## ⚠️ Common Issues & Troubleshooting

### ❌ Reviewer Not Assigned
- Incorrect configuration  

**Fix:** Verify reviewer rules

---

### ❌ Campaign Not Completed
- Reviewer delays  

**Fix:** Send reminders / escalate

---

### ❌ Revocation Not Working
- Provisioning issue  

**Fix:** Check provisioning plan

---

### ❌ Missing Audit Logs
- Logging misconfigured  

**Fix:** Enable audit tracking

---
